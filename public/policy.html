Privacy Policy for Step Counter: Daily Walk

Last updated: July 30, 2026


1. Introduction
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Step Counter: Daily Walk app ("the App") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information in compliance with applicable privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) and Google Play policies, including the Google Play Health Apps, Location Permissions, and Sensitive Data policies.
By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.


2. Your Activity and Route Data Stays on Your Device
Step Counter: Daily Walk is an offline-first fitness app. All of the activity data the App records is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

Step count, daily and historical step records, active time, and estimated calories burned

Your daily step goal and any profile values you enter, such as height, weight, gender, or age

Walk and route records, including the GPS path of a walk you choose to record, its distance, duration, pace, and the map view of that route

We do NOT upload, transmit, or store your step data, your walking routes, or your location history on our servers. We cannot access this data, and it is never sold or shared.


3. Location Data
The App uses your device's location only to draw the route of a walk while you are actively recording it, and to show that route on a map afterwards.

Location is accessed only while a walk is being recorded. When you stop the recording, the App stops accessing your location.

Recorded routes are saved on your device only. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Location data is never used for advertising, profiling, or resale, and is never shared with the advertising or analytics services listed in section 5.

You can use the App without granting the location permission. If you decline it, step counting, goals, and history all keep working — only the route-on-map feature is unavailable.

You can revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings, and you can delete individual recorded routes or your whole route history inside the App.


4. Information Collected by Third-Party Services
The App uses the Internet permission to display ads and collect basic, anonymous analytics that help us keep the App running and improve it. Through third-party SDKs, the following limited data may be collected automatically:

Device Info: Device model, operating system version, language

Advertising ID (GAID): Used by advertising and analytics services

App Usage & Diagnostics: Features used, app open frequency, and crash/performance logs

IP Address: Processed by advertising and analytics services, which may derive an approximate (city-level) region from it

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or photos. Your step counts, fitness data, and recorded GPS routes are never sent to these services.


5. Why This Data Is Collected
Show ads to support the free App (AdMob)

Measure app usage and improve features

Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and bugs


We do not sell personal data or use it for automated profiling with legal effects. We do not use your health, fitness, or location data for advertising purposes.


6. Third-Party Services
The App integrates the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

Google AdMob – advertising (https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Google Firebase – analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Facebook (Meta) SDK – advertising and analytics (https://www.facebook.com/policy.php)

AppsFlyer – attribution and analytics (https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/)

These partners comply with Google Play's data policies. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not share your physical activity or route data with them.


7. Permissions Used
ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION (Physical activity): Required to read step counts from your device's built-in step counter and motion sensors so the App can count your steps. This data is processed on your device and is used only to show you your own activity.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (Location): Required only to record the route of a walk and show it on a map, as described in section 3. Location is accessed only while you are recording a walk. This permission is optional — the App works without it.
INTERNET: Required to load ads and send anonymous analytics/crash reports.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required only to show your current step count in an ongoing notification and to send the goal reminders you turn on. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (and the location and health service types): Required so step counting and route recording can continue while the App is in the background or your screen is off.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required so step counting can resume after your device restarts.
The App does not request camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.


8. Health and Fitness Data
Your step count, activity records, and recorded routes are treated as sensitive information. We handle them as follows:

They are collected only to provide the App's core step-counting and walk-tracking features to you

They stay on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party

They are not used for advertising, profiling, or resale

The App does not connect to Google Fit, Health Connect, or any other external health platform, and does not read or write data in your device's health records.

The App is not a medical device. Step counts, distance, pace, and calorie figures are estimates produced by your device's sensors and are intended for general fitness and wellness purposes only. They are not medical advice and should not be used to diagnose or treat any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise programme.


9. Advertising
The App displays ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use the Advertising ID to show personalized or non-personalized ads. Where required by law, the App shows a consent form (Google UMP) so you can choose your ad preferences. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device's ad settings.
Your physical activity data and your recorded routes are never used to target ads.


10. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA)
Depending on your region, you have the right to:

Access, update, or delete data associated with you

Withdraw consent or revoke app permissions

Opt out of personalized advertising

Request information about the categories of data collected and shared

Because your step history, fitness records, and routes are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time inside the App or by uninstalling the App. You can also revoke the physical activity or location permission at any time in your device settings.


11. Data Retention
Your on-device step history, fitness data, and recorded routes remain until you delete them or uninstall the App.
Analytics and crash data collected by third-party services are typically retained for no longer than necessary for the purposes described above and in accordance with each provider's policy.


12. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.


13. Data Transfers
Data processed by third-party services may be stored or processed on servers outside your country. These providers implement safeguards in line with GDPR and other applicable laws.


14. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date will reflect the most recent update.
